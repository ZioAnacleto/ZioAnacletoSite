## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Astro (static output, TypeScript, built-in i18n routing) with plain scoped CSS and no UI framework. Chosen because the site is content-first, ships almost no JS, deploys as static files to Cloudflare Pages, and keeps EN/IT content in typed data files that are easy to maintain.

Deploy target: Cloudflare Pages with a custom domain.

## Users

A personal presence, not a conversion funnel. Visitors are anyone who looks Fabrizio up: colleagues, recruiters, people from the Turin music scene, friends. Their job is to understand, in a minute, who he is as an engineer and as a musician, and to find his other spaces (GitHub, LinkedIn, email).

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Fabrizio Traversa, Android Technical Leader based in Turin. It describes professional experience (banking and automotive Android), education, skills, and the parallel life as a percussionist and choir singer. Success: a visitor leaves with a clear, memorable picture of the person and knows where to go next.

## Positioning

Two disciplines held by one person: a conservatory-trained percussionist (8-year diploma) who is also an Android Technical Leader. Rhythm, timing and ensemble work on one side; architecture, code quality and team leadership on the other. No other engineer's portfolio can truthfully claim this pairing.
